# Pharmacy Management System
## Project Vision

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Initial Draft  
**Product Type:** Multi-Branch Pharmacy Management & ERP System

### 1. Product Overview

Pharmacy Management System is a comprehensive software platform designed to manage the daily operations of pharmacies, including medicine catalog management, inventory, batch tracking, sales, prescriptions, patients, suppliers, purchasing, financial operations, reporting, and branch management.

The platform will support both independent pharmacies and pharmacy chains through a configurable multi-branch architecture.

The system aims to improve operational efficiency, inventory accuracy, financial visibility, medicine traceability, and customer service.

### 2. Product Vision

Build a reliable, secure, scalable, and user-friendly pharmacy management platform that centralizes pharmacy operations and provides accurate, actionable information to pharmacy owners, managers, pharmacists, and employees.

### 3. Target Users

- Pharmacy Owners
- Branch Managers
- Pharmacists
- Cashiers
- Inventory Staff
- Accountants
- System Administrators

### 4. Business Objectives

1. Centralize pharmacy operations.
2. Maintain accurate medicine and inventory records.
3. Track medicine batches, expiry dates, and stock movements.
4. Streamline sales and prescription dispensing workflows.
5. Maintain patient profiles and prescription history.
6. Simplify purchasing and supplier management.
7. Provide financial and operational reporting.
8. Support multiple branches with centralized management.
9. Improve accountability through permissions and audit logs.
10. Support Arabic and English interfaces.

### 7. Initial Product Boundaries

The system is an operational management tool, not a replacement for professional medical judgment.

It must not independently diagnose patients, prescribe medication, or automatically approve prescription OCR results.

Drug interaction and allergy alerts are decision-support features requiring pharmacist review and reliable reference data.

Legal and regulatory requirements, including medicine classifications, prescription retention, controlled medicines, taxation, and personal data protection, must be validated against applicable Egyptian requirements before production deployment.

### 8. Success Criteria

- Accurate stock quantities across all branches.
- Traceable inventory movements.
- Reliable sales and return transactions.
- Correct batch and expiry tracking.
- Complete prescription and dispensing history.
- Secure role-based access.
- Consistent Arabic and English experiences.
- Reliable financial calculations.
- Automated and repeatable test coverage.
- Successful deployment with backup and recovery procedures.
